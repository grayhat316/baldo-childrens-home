Get involved

Money is the obvious thing. It is not the only thing, and for some people it is not the useful thing.

Give

Card or M-Pesa, any amount. If you want your gift tied to something specific, the three needs in the home's own paperwork are the kitchen, uniforms and secondary school fees.

Donate

Visit

The home is in Ndaragwa, on the equator 211 kilometres north-west of Nairobi, next to the Catholic parish. There is a guest house on the compound for visitors and volunteers.

Call before you come, on 0710 238 132. Not because visitors are unwelcome, but because a hundred children notice a stranger at the gate, and the sisters would rather know who is coming and why.

Volunteer

The home takes volunteers and attachments. What is useful depends on what you can actually do: teaching, farm work, repairs, sports, music, accountancy, or help with the paperwork that fundraising needs. Say what you can do and for how long, rather than what you would like to experience.

Anyone working with the children directly will be asked about safeguarding. That is normal, and it is the right answer to a stranger offering to spend time with children.

Give things, not money

Uniforms, cloth, school books, shoes, bedding, seeds, animal feed, a water tank, kitchen equipment. Ask first what is actually short, so that the home does not end up storing things it cannot use.

Parishes, companies and trusts

The most useful arrangement for a parish is a standing commitment rather than a collection, because school fees arrive three times a year whether or not there was a collection that month. A company can fund a specific thing and be credited for it on the partners page.

Write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com and say what you have in mind.
