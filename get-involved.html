Visiting

The home is in Ndaragwa, on the equator 211 kilometres north-west of Nairobi, next to the Catholic parish. There is a guest house on the compound for visitors and volunteers.

Call before you come. Not because visitors are unwelcome, but because a hundred children notice a stranger at the gate, and the sisters would rather know who is coming and why.

Volunteering

The home takes volunteers and attachments. What is useful depends on what you can actually do: teaching, farm work, repairs, sport, music, accountancy, or help with the paperwork fundraising needs.

Anyone working with the children directly will be asked about safeguarding. That is normal, and it is the right answer to a stranger offering to spend time with children.

For parishes, companies and trusts

The most useful arrangement for a parish is a standing commitment rather than a collection, because school fees arrive three times a year whether or not there was a collection that month.

A company can fund a specific thing and be credited for it on the partners page. A trust that needs accounts before it gives should ask the home for them first; that is a reasonable question and the answer exists.
