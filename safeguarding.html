Safeguarding and photographs

Last revised November 2026.

The short version: no child is named on this website, no face is published with a name, and photographs go up only with written consent from the manager and the child where they are old enough to give it.

This is not decoration. A children's home that lists its children creates a risk that did not exist before, and a child who later goes back to family should not have to live with a permanent public page about the worst year of their life.

What has to happen before photographs go up

Written consent from the home's manager for each photograph, and, where a child is identifiable and old enough to understand, that child's own agreement. Consent can be withdrawn. If it is, the picture comes down.

Anyone taking photographs at the home, including visitors and volunteers, should be working to the home's own safeguarding policy. Publish your pictures only with the manager's written permission.

Visitors and volunteers

Anyone who will spend time with the children should expect to be asked who they are, what they will be doing, and for how long. References may be requested. Anyone unwilling to answer those questions should not be working with children anywhere.

If you are worried about a child

Speak to the home directly on 0710 238 132, or send a message on WhatsApp. In Kenya, concerns about a child can also go to the local children's office or the police. Do not post a concern about a named child on this site or on social media.

Who to ask

The home's full safeguarding policy is held at Ndaragwa and can be requested from the manager. Anyone who wants to see it before visiting or volunteering is welcome to ask.
