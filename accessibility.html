Accessibility

Last revised 1 November 2026. Written for the demonstration site.

What was done

Pages are plain HTML with real headings in order, so a screen reader can navigate them. Every form field has a label. The donate buttons announce whether they are selected. Focus outlines are left visible. Text and background colours were chosen to pass contrast checks at body size. The site works without JavaScript for reading; only the amount buttons and the film need it.

Known gaps

Photographs carry short descriptions rather than long ones. The film is on YouTube and its own captions depend on what the uploader provided, which for a 2015 recording may be nothing. The amount buttons are small on a narrow phone screen.

Text size

The site uses relative sizes, so your browser's zoom and your own font size setting both work. Nothing breaks at 200 per cent.

If something does not work

Write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com and say which page and what happened. Accessibility problems reported are worth fixing properly rather than patched around.

Note

This page describes what has been built. It is not a claim of conformance with any standard, because no independent audit has been done.
