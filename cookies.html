Cookie notice

Last revised 1 November 2026. Written for the demonstration site.

The short version

This site does not set cookies. It does not use analytics, so there is nothing to consent to and no banner asking you to.

What happens when you donate

Pressing continue sends you to the payment provider. On their domain they will set what they need to run a secure transaction: a session cookie, fraud checks, and the records they are required to keep. Those cookies belong to the provider, not to this site, and their own notice applies once you are there.

The film

The video on the home page is not loaded when the page opens. The image you see is a still. The YouTube player is added only after you press play, and it uses the no-cookie domain so that YouTube does not set advertising cookies before you interact. If you never press play, YouTube is never contacted.

Fonts and images

Fonts are the ones already on your device. Images are served from this site. There is no third party content delivery network setting anything.

If you change your mind

Because this site sets no cookies, there is nothing here to withdraw. To clear anything the payment provider left, clear cookies for its domain in your browser settings, or use a private window. Blocking third party cookies in your browser will not break the donate form.
