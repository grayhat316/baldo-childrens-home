Donate

Card from anywhere in the world, or M-Pesa if you are in Kenya. Pick one of the amounts below or put in your own.

Give once

Card payments are handled by Paystack, which takes Visa, Mastercard and Verve, and by Apple Pay on a phone. M-Pesa goes through the same provider as a mobile money charge, so you will get the prompt on your handset and confirm it with your PIN.

The home is a registered community based organisation under Ndaragwa Catholic Parish. Test mode is on for this demonstration, so no money moves.

Card details go to the payment provider, never to this site. See the donation policy and the privacy notice.

What happens after you give

You get an emailed receipt from the payment provider with a reference number. The money lands in the home's account. If you gave by M-Pesa, the confirmation SMS from Safaricom is your receipt as well.

If something goes wrong, a card is declined, a prompt does not arrive, or a charge goes through twice, write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com with the reference and the date. Refunds and duplicate charges are handled under the donation policy.

Other ways to give

For a bank transfer, a cheque, or a gift of goods rather than money, contact the home first so the payment can be tracked against the right thing. The sisters keep a record of what comes in and what it was given for.

If you are giving from outside Kenya and want a tax receipt in your own country, say so when you write. There are existing partner organisations in Italy and in Britain that can route a gift and issue the paperwork. Details are on the partners page.

Where it is most needed

  • Secondary school fees, uniforms and books.
  • A replacement for the kitchen.
  • Water storage, because the supply reaches the compound about twice a week.
  • Medicine and clinic costs.

Questions people ask before giving

Is this the official site of the home?

Not yet. This is a demonstration put together from the home's own documents and from what partner organisations have published. The home has not adopted it. Nothing here charges a card.

Will I be signed up to anything?

No. There is no newsletter, no mailing list, and no account to create. Give your email for the receipt and that is all it is used for.

Can I give to one particular child?

Ask the home. Sponsorship of an individual child is a conversation with the sisters, not a checkout, because it involves a child's privacy.
