Donate

Card from anywhere in the world, or M-Pesa if you are in Kenya. Every shilling goes to the home's account and is spent on food, school fees, uniforms and medicine.

Give once

Card payments take Visa, Mastercard and Verve, and Apple Pay on a phone. M-Pesa goes through as a mobile money charge, so you will get the prompt on your handset and confirm it with your PIN.

The home is a registered community based organisation under Ndaragwa Catholic Parish, Ndaragwa, Nyandarua County.

Donation policy   Privacy notice

Your card details are entered on the payment provider's secure page and are never seen or stored by this site.

What happens after you give

You get an email with a receipt and a reference number. If you gave by M-Pesa, the Safaricom message on your phone is a receipt as well. Keep either one.

If a card is declined, a prompt does not arrive, or a charge goes through twice, write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com with the reference and the date. Duplicate and unauthorised charges are refunded.

Other ways to give

For a bank transfer, a cheque, or a gift of goods rather than money, contact the home first so it can be tracked against the right thing. Partner organisations in Italy and Britain can route a gift and issue local paperwork; the partners page names them.

Where it is most needed

  • Secondary school feesFees, uniforms and books.
  • A replacement kitchenNamed in the home's own list of needs.
  • Water storageThe supply reaches the compound twice a week.
  • Medicine and clinic costsFor around a hundred children.

Where your money goes

Questions people ask before giving

Is my payment secure?

Card details go directly to the payment provider, never to this site, and nothing about your card is stored here. The connection is encrypted end to end.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes, by email, straight away. M-Pesa donors also get the Safaricom confirmation.

Can I give every month?

A standing monthly gift is best arranged directly with the home, so that it can be recorded against school terms. Write to the sisters and they will set it up.

Can I sponsor one particular child?

That is a conversation with the home rather than a checkout, because it involves a child's privacy. Call 0710 238 132 or write.

Will my donation be tax deductible?

That depends on the home's registration and on the law where you pay tax. Ask for a receipt and check with your own adviser. The partner organisations in Italy and Britain may be able to issue local paperwork.

Not sure which amount is useful?

Ask the home what a school term costs, or what the kitchen needs. They will tell you plainly.
