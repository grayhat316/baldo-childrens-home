Donate to the home

Any amount is useful. The home spends it on food, school fees, uniforms and medicine, and your receipt comes by email immediately.

Give once

Cards take Visa, Mastercard and Verve, and Apple Pay on a phone. M-Pesa is a mobile money charge: you enter the number, a prompt arrives on that handset, and you confirm it with your PIN.

The home is a registered community based organisation under Ndaragwa Catholic Parish, Ndaragwa, Nyandarua County.

After you give

  1. Receipt by emailWith a reference number. M-Pesa donors also get the Safaricom confirmation.
  2. The money reaches the home's accountLess the provider's fee. Card payments settle through the bank, M-Pesa through the mobile money settlement.
  3. It is spent, and recordedThe sisters keep a record of what came in and what it was given for. Ask for that record at any time.

If a card is declined, a prompt does not arrive, or a charge lands twice, write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com with the reference and the date. Duplicate and unauthorised charges are refunded.

  • Secondary school feesFees, uniforms and books, three terms a year.

Where your money goes

Before you give

Is my payment secure?

Card details go directly to the payment provider, never to this site, and nothing about your card is stored here. The connection is encrypted end to end.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes, by email, straight away. M-Pesa donors also get the Safaricom confirmation, which is equally valid.

Can I give every month?

A standing monthly gift is best arranged with the home directly, so it can be recorded against school terms. Write to the sisters and they will set it up.

Can I sponsor one particular child?

That is a conversation with the home rather than a checkout, because it involves a child's privacy. Call or message 0710 238 132 or write.

Will my donation be tax deductible?

That depends on the home's registration and on the law where you pay tax. Ask for a receipt and check with your own adviser. Partner organisations in Italy and Britain may be able to issue local paperwork.

What if money leaves my account and no receipt arrives?

Do not give again. Write with the date, the amount and any reference, and it will be traced.

Not sure which amount is useful?

Ask what a school term costs, or what the kitchen needs. The sisters will tell you plainly.
