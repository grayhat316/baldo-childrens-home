Accountability

Last revised November 2026.

The short version: this site publishes no financial figures because the sources it was built from disagree. The home can give you its budget, its accounts and its registration number when you ask.

What should be published here

Once a site is adopted, this page should carry four things, and none of them should have to be asked for:

  • The CBO registration number and the date of registration.
  • Annual income and expenditure, signed by whoever keeps the books.
  • A short report on each funded project, with the amount spent and what it bought.
  • The name of the person who authorises payments, and how a complaint reaches them.

None of that is on this page yet. It has to come from the home, and inventing it would defeat the purpose.

How the money is handled now

Donations are managed for the home by partner organisations, notably Ruota Amica in Italy, and pass through the home's own account for local spending. The sisters keep a record of what comes in and what it was given for. The farm's surplus is sold at the market in Ndaragwa and pays the water and electricity bills, which means there is a written trail for those too.

If something looks wrong

Ask. Write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com and ask for the entry in the accounts that covers your gift. A charity that cannot answer that question has a problem, and the asking is not rude.

On the figures published here

This site gives the number of children as around a hundred. Organisations that have written about the home in different years put it at 130, 161 and 80, which is why a single precise figure is not published. If you need an exact number for a grant application, ask the home.
