Privacy notice

Last revised 1 November 2026. Written for the demonstration site.

Who this covers

This is a demonstration website prepared for Fr. Baldo Children's Home in Ndaragwa, Kenya. It is not the home's official site and it does not take money. When the home adopts a site, this notice has to be rewritten to name the organisation that is actually collecting donations, with its registration number and a physical address.

What the site collects

Nothing on its own. There is no analytics, no tracking script, no advertising pixel and no account to create.

If you fill in the donate form, these values go to the payment provider when you press continue: the amount, the currency, the method, and the email address you typed for the receipt. The card number and the M-Pesa confirmation happen on the provider's own systems. They are never sent to this site and never stored by it.

Cookies

This site sets none. See the cookie notice for what the payment provider and the video player do on their own domains.

Who else is involved

Payments are processed by a payment provider, currently Paystack, acting as the processor. Their privacy notice governs the card or mobile money part of the transaction. The film on the home page is hosted by YouTube and is only contacted after you press play.

How long it is kept

Receipt details are kept by the payment provider according to its own schedule, and by the home for as long as its accounts require. A Kenyan charity needs to keep financial records for several years, so a receipt is not deleted when you stop thinking about it.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, subject to the record keeping the home has to do. Write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com. Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 applies, and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner is the regulator.

Children

This site is not directed at children and does not ask them for anything. No information about any child at the home appears here. The safeguarding page explains that rule.
