Thank you

Your donation has been received.

An email is on its way with your receipt and a reference number. Keep it: it is what the home needs if a payment ever has to be traced. If you gave by M-Pesa, the Safaricom message on your phone is your receipt too.

What your money does

Food the farm does not produce, school fees for the coming term, uniforms, and medicine. If you asked for your gift to go to something specific, the sisters will use it for that and tell you when it has been spent.

If anything about the payment looks wrong, write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com with the reference and the date rather than giving again.
