Donation policy

Last revised November 2026.

Before you give

This policy covers donations made through this website, by card or by M-Pesa. It sets out how a gift is processed, what you receive, and what happens when something goes wrong.

How a gift is processed

Card payments and M-Pesa charges are handled by a payment provider acting as processor. The home never sees or stores a card number. The money settles into the home's account, less the provider's fee.

Receipts

You get an emailed receipt with a reference. For M-Pesa, the Safaricom confirmation message is also your record. Keep either one: it is what the home needs to trace a payment that goes wrong.

Refunds

A donation is not a purchase, so there is no change of mind return. Two exceptions are handled without argument: a charge made twice by mistake, and a charge you did not authorise. Write to baldochildrenshome@gmail.com with the reference and the date, and a duplicate or unauthorised charge is refunded.

When something fails

A declined card, an M-Pesa prompt that never arrives, or a payment that leaves your account without a receipt are all fixable with the reference number. Do not send a second payment before writing.

Currency and fees

You choose the currency. The provider converts, and may charge a cross border fee on a card issued outside Kenya. The amount the home receives is the amount you gave, less the provider's fee.

Tax

No tax deduction is promised. Whether a gift is deductible depends on the home's registration and on the law where you pay tax. Ask for a receipt and check with your own adviser. Existing partner organisations in Italy and Britain may be able to route a gift and issue local paperwork.

Restricted gifts

If you give for something specific, the kitchen, uniforms or school fees, the home will use it for that. If the need is met, the sisters will tell you and ask what you would prefer rather than quietly spending it elsewhere.
