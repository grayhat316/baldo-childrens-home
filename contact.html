Finding it

Ndaragwa is in Nyandarua County, on the equator, 211 kilometres north-west of Nairobi. The home is next to the Catholic parish in the township.

Call before travelling. If you are coming from outside Kenya, ask what the road is like at that time of year before you set out.

For anything about a donation already made, have the reference number from your receipt to hand.

About this website

This is a demonstration site, put together for the home from its own documents and from what partner organisations have published. It is not the home's official site, and it does not collect money. If the home adopts it, this page will carry their updates instead of this note.
