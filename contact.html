Finding it

Ndaragwa is in Nyandarua County, on the equator. The home sits next to the Catholic parish in the township.

Call before travelling. If you are coming from outside Kenya, ask what the road is like at that time of year before you set out.

If you are coming to volunteer, say so when you call, and say what you can do. It saves a conversation at the gate.
