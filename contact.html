Find us

Ndaragwa is in Nyandarua County, 211 kilometres north-west of Nairobi, on the road between Nyeri and Nyahururu. The home sits beside the Catholic parish in the township.

Baldo Childrens Home, Nyeri-Nyahururu Road, Gwakungu, Ndaragwa. Coordinates -0.0525511, 36.5266713. Open in Google Maps for directions.

If you are coming

Ring the home before you travel, on 0710 238 132, or send a WhatsApp message, and say when you expect to arrive and what you are coming for. The sisters plan the day around the children, and a visitor who arrives unannounced is harder to fit in than one they know about.

There is a guest house on the compound, used by volunteers and by visitors who are staying more than a day. Ask about it in the same call.

Anyone who will spend time with the children is asked about safeguarding first. That applies to volunteers, to film crews and to donors who want to meet the children they have supported. It is not suspicion, it is the rule the home runs to.
